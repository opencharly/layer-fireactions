**The snapshotter chain is the reason the containerd commits exist.** `opencharly/fireactions` accepts only

pinned tag **neither was served** — so no configuration of this consumer could start.

created and formatted before the daemon starts), landed, and tagged `v2026.289.0839`; this PR's pin bump
consumes it and declares `containerd: {address, namespace, snapshotter: blockfile}`.
